Good to know

A few quick answers.

How do I arrange a ride?

Call (832) 754-6480. Have the appointment date, pickup location and destination ready. Dispatch will review the details and confirm availability with you.

Can you help with recurring dialysis appointments?

Yes. Speak with dispatch about your treatment days and appointment times so the team can discuss a recurring transportation plan.

Can a family member or care team contact you?

Yes. We welcome calls from patients, family members, caregivers and referring care teams. Call dispatch to discuss the next steps.

What about payment or insurance?

Ask dispatch about payment options and any eligibility or authorization requirements before scheduling. Coverage depends on the individual arrangement.
