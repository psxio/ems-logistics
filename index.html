A family-led approach.
A neighborly kind of care.

Personal care. Local connections.

We’re building EMS Logistics around the people behind each appointment—the patient getting ready, the family helping out, and the care team expecting them.

Our role would be to help coordinate the details and keep everyone informed. A qualified transportation partner would provide the vehicles, staff and rides.

We’re starting small in Houston, with a focus on recurring dialysis transportation and straightforward, personal communication.

Good to know

A few quick answers.

Can I book a ride now?

Real bookings are not open yet. You can try the sample request flow and see how a coordinator would review and schedule it. No real ride is booked.

Who would provide the transportation?

A qualified transportation partner would provide the vehicles, staff and rides. EMS Logistics would help coordinate the request and schedule.

Would insurance cover the ride?

Coverage is not guaranteed. The partner would confirm eligibility, authorization and any costs before scheduling.
