The planned experience

Three simple steps.

  1. 1

    Talk with us

    A patient, family member or care team asks for help arranging transportation.

  2. 2

    Make a ride plan

    The transportation partner confirms needs, availability and payment before scheduling.

  3. 3

    Get there and back

    The partner provides the ride. Our team helps coordinate the schedule and any changes.

Good to know

A few quick answers.

Can I book a ride now?

Real bookings are not open yet. You can try the sample ride-request flow and see how a coordinator would review and schedule it. The demo does not send a request or arrange transportation.

Who would provide the transportation?

A qualified transportation partner would provide the vehicles, staff and rides. EMS Logistics would help with coordination.

Would insurance cover the ride?

Coverage is not guaranteed. The partner would confirm eligibility, authorization and any costs before scheduling.
